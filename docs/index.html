What the application covers

Demand forecasting

Daily forecasts for four SKUs with a trained v3 stacking ensemble and planner overrides.

Material planning

BOM-driven requirements, editable safety stock, MOQ rounding, costs, and lead-time warnings.

Order execution

Place orders, update status, and automatically reflect received materials in stock.
